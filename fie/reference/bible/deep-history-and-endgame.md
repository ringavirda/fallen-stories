# Deep History, the Riders & the Planned Endgame

This layer sits *beneath* everything in [worldbuilding.md](worldbuilding.md): it is the true
prehistory of ponies, the eschatology of the Four Riders, and the planned shape of the whole saga
through Fragments II–III. It also folds the ponies firmly into the angel/Lilith cosmology — ponies
are **the Creator's own descendants**, not an incidental race.

## A. The Four Civilizations (prehistory) — `First-ponies.md`

**1. The First Civilization.** Ponies are the **true descendants of the Creator (нащадки Творця)**,
made to be *keepers of the garden* (Harmony) that was meant as a gift for **Lilith**. But the
Betrayal and the War of the Angels shattered that garden; the first ponies awoke into a fractured
reality of magic-storms. These first ponies were **heavy, muscular, and could not touch magic** —
they were *enemies of magic*, unable to exist where it concentrated. In Lilith's dark-fire storms
the first **shadows / nightmares (тіні, кошмари)** formed and devoured pony "oases" — the first war
inside Harmony. Ponies answered with **logic and technology**, building a world-creating-and-
destroying empire. Their masterwork: the **Культиватори (Cultivators)** — constructs of information
and energy that **stabilize reality, create worlds, and link them** by tunnels and threads,
establishing the fabric that walls the nightmares out — **the divide between reality and dreams**.
- But the fire of creation carried Lilith's power and could not be destroyed. Reality's attempt to
  purge it birthed **«Смерть» (Death)** — revealed to be **the imprint of the Creator's own
  consciousness, driven mad by the horror of the Betrayal**, now hating and seeking to destroy
  everything Lilith made — including the ponies, his own children.

**2. Fall of the Empire.** Even the Creator's "pure children" carried a spark of Lilith's magic,
which grew over generations until the ponies' own **anti-magic Cultivators identified them as the
enemy** and turned on them — freezing or burning their worlds, collapsing the inter-world tunnels.
Near-total extinction; survivors hid in caves and oceans and **lost almost all knowledge**. Surviving
shards left working machines no modern pony understands. One shard: the **Crystal Empire** (its people
forgot their nature and became **crystal ponies**). The dormant machines that kept the surface
uninhabitable — their true name forgotten — are the **Windigos (Вендіго)**. Eventually drained, they
fell into sleep, buried by sand and time, letting new life arise. **→ The Windigos ARE the Cultivators.**

**3. The Second Civilization.** With Harmony now stable and walled from dreams, the Creator's original
design finally bloomed: new life carrying the **mark of fire** (stronger toward Harmony's center).
The re-emerged ponies met a magic-drenched world that reshaped them — losing the old muscular bodies,
becoming **soft and graceful**, splitting by magical affinity into the modern tribes (**earth** = least
magical, most like the originals; **pegasi/sea ponies** = flight/water; **unicorns** = fused with magic,
gained the horn). They forgot their history and warred — until **Grogar** (one of the first dark mages,
so magic-saturated he warped existence) craved their creative power and forged an army of monsters and
hybrids, his masterpiece being the **Changelings and their Queen** (corrupted ponies, the ultimate
anti-pony weapon). Unleashing the swarm forced the tribes to **unite** into the (First-really-Second)
kingdom that defeated and de-powered him.

**4. Second Fall → Third State.** Proud unicorns dug beneath their capital into the **First Empire's
ruins**; the ancient security systems mistook these visually-different unicorns for **void-nightmares**
and **reactivated the Cultivators/Windigos**, freezing the old homeland into an anti-magic desert.
**Queen Platinum** destroyed the evidence (and the researchers) and fled south. The refugees seized new
land — **Equestria** — using their monster-war weapons (dark magic, hybrids), and formed the **Коло
(Circle) of higher unicorns**, a collective voting government meant to prevent one pony from endangering
all again.
- The new land needed reshaping; earth ponies and pegasi seized the ecosystems and weather, unicorns
  bent the day/night cycles. But pony **aggression** kept driving toward apocalypse. The **Pillars of
  Equestria** and the Circle fought catastrophe after catastrophe, concluding the flaw was **not magic
  but ponies themselves**. The Circle's answer used the ordinary craft of making **magical hybrids** (the
  same process behind cockatrices, bugbears, etc.) — but pushed to a **perfect fusion** of the three
  tribes, far deeper than any crude hybrid. Crude hybrids are mad, destructive, and only follow a master's
  will; this perfect fusion instead created **true, genuinely new life — something not originally part of
  the world, and therefore extraordinarily powerful**. That is the Circle's real atrocity. They meant the
  **alicorns** as obedient tools ("automatons") to maintain pony existence, but two (**Celestia & Luna**)
  awoke **full consciousness and their own ideas**. When the Pillars fell to the **Shadow Pony (Тіньовий
  поні)**, the sisters seized control, judged the Circle corrupt, and **rewrote the world** to their
  vision. *(So "fusion of the tribes" and "artificial instruments" are the same act — the distinction that
  matters is crude hybrid vs. perfect, self-aware new life.)*

**5. Fall of the Sisters → Fourth Kingdom.** The sisters — effective, not cruel — **systematically
exterminated dark magic's bearers and all memory of the craft**, monopolizing high sorcery, and named
themselves **princesses**. In the conflict with **King Sombra** (last great dark mage), Luna was
infected by a **higher shadow** (an otherworldly "virus") that twisted her into the sisters' war;
Celestia sealed her in the **imprint of the Moon**. Day then ruled alone over a **thousand years of
peace** — until new princesses appeared (**Love → Friendship → Destruction**), whose conflict tore the
world again and nearly released the ponies' original enemies, the **nightmares from beyond**. *(This is
where the written story — Fragment I — begins.)*

## B. The Four Riders — `Riders.md`

Forces that can end all existence, with four names/forms; their coming heralds the true End:

| # | Name (form) | Bearer | Artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Самотність (Loneliness)** — the root; alone, you meet your own fanged reflection | the **erased First Rider** (life & memory erased from reality by the *past* Princess of Time) | **Amulet of the Alicorn** (power of **Викривлення / Distortion**) |
| 2 | **Анігіляція (Annihilation)** — the fire that leaves no ash | **Sunset Shimmer** | the **fire of creation** (create anything from imagination) |
| 3 | **Порожнеча (Void)** — what remains after the dark fire, giving no reflection | **Fallenstar** | the sword **Слабкість (Weakness)** — cuts reality *and the meaning of existence* |
| 4 | **The Unknown** — no one knows its face; that ignorance is the *sign that salvation is still possible* | **Twilight Ash** (echo of the erased past; **leader** of the Riders; **Princess of Time**) | the **Sands of Time (Піски Часу)** — she remembers all; what she forgets ceases to exist |

The legend ponies know is a lie: the First Rider truly existed in the forgotten past. Twilight Ash, the
last Rider, **already arrived at the end of the war with the shadows** — so the "victory" and world-
restoration are only the **start of the countdown to the End**. Amid the storm woke the ancient
invincible enemy — **Death** (the mad Creator) — snuffing out worlds and stars in pursuit of all living
souls. The Riders are the only force that can **slow Death**, buying ponies time to find a way to stop
the true Kінець. *(Note the tie-in: Fallenstar's Book-1 farewell line "don't fear your own reflection"
is the Loneliness/Rider motif seeded early.)*

**Leadership & morality.** **Twilight Ash leads** the Riders; **Fallenstar is her Rider** — she mostly
follows Ash's will, destroying whatever is necessary to slow the enemy. That duty cuts both ways: **defending
worlds** *and* **carrying out death sentences** on what can no longer be saved. The Riders are **neither good
nor evil** — they simply do what is necessary to save what is left. ("Fallenstar is *the* Rider of Twilight
Ash" and "there are four Riders" are both true: Ash is the leader/executor of fate; the others carry out her
will.)

## Scope — Death, Lilith & the larger universe

**Death** (the mad Creator's consciousness) is the antagonist of *this* saga. **Lilith and the Creator
themselves are concepts of the wider universe** and are **not directly present** in the current story — their
only in-story footprint is that the **sands of time** (from the Creator) and the **fire of creation** (from
Lilith) originate with them. **Lilith becomes relevant only in a possible continuation:** once **Death is
dealt with, the Harmony storm subsides**, opening this star-cluster to the rest of the galaxy — and, sitting
**between two warring galactic arms**, the ponies would then have to **defend their home once more**. So Death
and Lilith are not a contradiction; they are **two threats at two scales/times** (internal-origin endgame vs.
external-future sequel hook).

## C. The Planned Arc / Endgame — `Timeline.md`

The intended shape of the whole saga (Fragment I finishes the first movements; II "Time" and III
"Eternity" cover the rest):

1. Fallenstar rises against the princesses → the **Rebellion / cataclysm / shadow-invasion**.
2. **Moonlight Shadow** becomes regent of Equestria, holding back the nightmares.
3. Fallenstar finds **Eterna's** memory (the first alicorn of Time) and learns the world's true nature:
   **an alicorn's dream**.
4. The last forces of the Fallen + Equestria punch into the **center of the Порожнеча (Void)**, letting
   Fallenstar use **Слабкість** to break the cycle and reach the **sleeping alicorn**.
5. The **Twilight of the perished world** (the sleeping alicorn) shares her memory: the *real* world
   died in the fire of a rebellion **led by Fallenstar**, who used **techno-magic** to distort Harmony
   and **burned Twilight's friends as fuel** — the conflict that ended that world.
6. That past-Twilight received the **Sands of Time + Fire of Creation** from **Eterna** and the **Sunset
   Shimmer of the past world**; now she and Fallenstar use them to **break the dream and restore reality**.
7. The princesses convene, set the new world's laws; **Fallenstar abdicates Destruction to Moonlight
   Shadow**, who leads the Fallen north to found the **Dominion of Destruction** in the dead lands of
   the ponies' **first homeland**.
8. **Chrysalis** leads a changed swarm against New Equestria, forcing Destruction's forces back to fight
   alongside the princesses — restoring the balance and healing the old Equestria-vs-Fallen hatred.
9. Moonlight later revives two frozen ponies in the ancient unicorn palace; from these first-civilization
   researchers the Fallen learn the Windigos' true nature and, via a princess's power, **contact the AI
   of the buried city (a Cultivator)**.
10. Using the AI, they **take control of the Windigos**, restore life to the dead land, and open a path
    to the stars.
11. They build the starship **Крило Принцеси (Wing of the Princess)**, which uses **Cultivators** to open
    subspace tunnels; its first star-jump through the storm/void succeeds via **the Riders' support**.
12. Exploring worlds, they find the **first civilization's station** that once studied **Death**; reaching
    it (aboard the **Крило Янгола / Wing of the Angel**), they fight the station's anti-Death AI (which
    marks the princesses as its target), seize it, and **launch a protocol that halts Death for a time**.
13. Station logs reveal a **cryo-archive of living first-ponies**; they revive them.
14. First-civilization ponies, second-kingdom ponies, the princesses, and modern ponies — armed with
    techno-magic — begin the **final war: to overcome the Creator's hatred for his own creation (Death).**
