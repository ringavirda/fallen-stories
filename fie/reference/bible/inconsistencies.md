# Open inconsistencies & canon-in-flux

Conflicts found across the corpus. None are errors exactly — most reflect your worldbuilding being
mid-revision — but they're worth resolving before the Ukrainian text locks in canon.

## Cosmology — two coexisting layers (now clearly ONE, revealed in stages)
- **[canon, UK appendices]** treat magic's origin as *unknowable* and princesses as demystified-but-
  unexplained; `Про природу магії` never mentions angels.
- **[revision]** `О космологии` / `Cosmology.md` propose a hard-SF **false-angels / Lilith / Tree of
  Memory** origin (explicitly modeled on Warhammer 40k's C'tan).
- **`First-ponies.md` now commits to the angel layer as the backbone:** ponies are literally the
  **Creator's descendants**, «Смерть»/Death is the **mad Creator**, the Windigos are the first ponies'
  machines, etc. So this is no longer two rival cosmologies — it's **one cosmology revealed progressively**
  (folk myth → schoolbook → the deep truth in `First-ponies`/`Riders`/Strange Tales).
- **Remaining decision:** how much of the deep layer to surface in the UK appendices vs. keep as a reveal.
  The UK `Про природу магії` / `Пам'ять` are written as *in-world documents that don't know the truth* —
  which is internally consistent, as long as that's intentional.

## Wendigo origin — ✅ LARGELY RESOLVED (`First-ponies.md`)
- **Newest canon:** the Windigos **ARE the Cultivators (Культиватори)** — ancient anti-magic war-machines
  of the first pony civilization that turned on their makers, later dormant, and reawaken when triggered
  (e.g. by Platinum's unicorns disturbing the First Empire's ruins). See
  [deep-history-and-endgame.md](deep-history-and-endgame.md).
- This supersedes and re-frames the older versions: **[UK, `Пам'ять`]** "spirits drawn by discord" is the
  garbled folk-memory; **[`О космологии`]** "the Time-princess sent them south" and the Rebellion arc's
  "Guardian of Fate / Eradication" are partial truths. *Left to do:* align the UK `Пам'ять` appendix with
  the Cultivator canon (or keep it deliberately as the in-world myth).

## How alicorns are made — ✅ RESOLVED (`First-ponies.md`)
- **Newest canon:** the **Circle of unicorns manufactured the alicorns** as fused-tribe "magical
  automatons" meant to maintain pony existence; **Celestia & Luna unexpectedly awoke full consciousness**
  and overthrew the Circle. This harmonizes the earlier two accounts: **[UK, `Пам'ять`]** "fusion of the
  three tribes" = how the *body* is made, and **[`Princess guide`]** "touching an aspect / not the same as
  a princess" = how one *becomes a princess*. Both are now compatible.

## Fallenstar's exit from Equestria
- **Old version** (rejected in `Cosmology.md`): her body "degraded in Equestria so she left."
- **[revision]**: she becomes **Twilight Ash's Rider** and leaves to guard all worlds. Make sure the written
  finale uses the revised reason.

## Power-letter scale (character strength)
- Fallenstar's ascended form is **"omega"** in the character list but **"alpha"** at the Rebellion in the
  same document set.
- The **shield-class Greek scale** in `Equipment.md` runs **alpha (weak) → iota (strong)** — the *opposite*
  polarity to the character scale. Pick one convention (and one direction) and apply it everywhere.

## Notation speaker-code collisions
- `[Св]` = **Strike Vortex** *and* **Swift Voice**.
- `[Са]` = **Silent (Сайлент)** *and* (in drafts) **Saari**.
- `[Сг]` = **Starlight Glimmer** (glossary) *and* **Starfall Glow** (the mother, in `Пам'ять`).
- The glossary itself is the right place to fix these; consider unique 2–3 letter codes.

## "First alicorn" — Eterna vs the Circle's alicorns — ✅ RESOLVED (author)
- **Eterna was a *princess*, not (merely) an alicorn.** She **created the first throne**, anchoring the
  concept of Time. "Alicorn" is the *body-form*; a *princess* is the aspect-embodiment. So there is no
  contradiction with the Circle's later **artificial** alicorns (Celestia/Luna) — those are alicorn-form
  automatons, whereas Eterna is the primordial Time-*princess*. (And "fusion of tribes" = "artificial
  instruments": the same hybridization craft, distinguished only by crude hybrid vs. *perfect, self-aware
  new life*.) See [worldbuilding.md §3](worldbuilding.md#3-princesses--thrones) and
  [deep-history-and-endgame.md](deep-history-and-endgame.md).

## Riders framework — ✅ RESOLVED (author)
- Both framings are true: **Twilight Ash leads** the four Riders and **Fallenstar is her Rider** (follows
  Ash's will). Riders are **amoral** — they do what's necessary (defend worlds *and* pass death sentences).
  See [deep-history-and-endgame.md §B](deep-history-and-endgame.md#b-the-four-riders--ridersmd).

## Death vs. Lilith — ✅ RESOLVED (author)
- **Different scales/times, not a contradiction.** **Death** (the mad Creator) is *this* saga's endgame
  antagonist. **Lilith & the Creator are larger-universe concepts** not directly in-story — their only
  footprint is the origin of the **sands of time** & **fire of creation**. Lilith is a **sequel hook**: once
  Death is beaten, the Harmony storm subsides and the cluster (between two warring galactic arms) opens to
  the galaxy, forcing ponies to defend their home again.

## The three "Twilight/Time" figures — clarified (still worth a glossary line to avoid reader confusion)
Distinct, and now mechanically connected (see [worldbuilding.md §3](worldbuilding.md#3-princesses--thrones)):
- **Eterna** — the *original* Princess of Time (created the first throne); **dead**. Her killing plunged the
  world into the dream. Only a **memory-image** of her survives inside the dream, because the dreaming
  Twilight can't imagine a world without her.
- **Old-world (sleeping) Twilight** — the "Sleeping Princess" / sleeping alicorn whose dream *is* the story
  world; she holds the sands + fire she received from Eterna and the past-world Sunset.
- **Twilight Ash** — that same old-world Twilight *after* she takes the vacant Throne of Time (re-synchronizing
  time across worlds) and becomes leader of the Riders.
The three are one lineage of the Time-aspect; a glossary note spelling this out would still help readers.
