# Synopsis

Protagonist naming across the book: **Fading Starfall / Зникаючий Зорепад / «Фейд»** (child) →
**Fallenstar / Фолленстар** (after the Amulet) → cover identity **Starry Night / Зоряна Ніч**
→ self-styled **Fallen Star / Павша Зірка → Cursed Star / Проклята Зірка**.

## BOOK 1 «Слід павшої Зірки» (Trail of the Fallen Star) — translated & complete

### Ч1 «Зникаючий Зорепад» (Fading Starfall)
- **Р0 Легенда про Принцесу** — mythic overture (not tied to characters): an old soul in a young
  pony's body seizes princess-only tools and breaks the world; the princess pities rather than
  punishes her; defeated, the princess sheds "tear-stars" and retreats into a cycle of dreams.
  "Awakening is near."
- **Р1 Забутий сон** — the filly's recurring dream; a blind zebra at a Canterlot fair tells the
  legend of the last battle of five alicorn embodiments (Light, Dark, Love, Time, Friendship),
  where the enemy turned the heroes' own love/friendship against them via a corrupted machine,
  and Time arrived to *renew* rather than destroy. She wakes as "Starfall."
- **Р2 Перші питання** — backstory: the aristocratic earth-pony **Starfall** family are court
  astronomers who calibrate Equestria's cycles for the princesses. Fade is a curious, isolated
  prodigy; gets her cutie mark (blue comets) misusing a telescope; becomes fixated on the
  "impossible physics" of alicorn power and the total scholarly silence around it.
- **Р3 Ідеальний світ** — her research into "what existed before the alicorns" hits a wall of
  suppressed information; she turns to black-market relic hunters (incl. a real **Daring Do**),
  sets up a covert base in an abandoned observatory, and acquires a Sombra-era Crystal Empire
  marble fragment she wears ever after.
- **Р4 Проклятий дарунок** — she hears of the cursed **Amulet of the Alicorn**, steals it from a
  nervous amateur thief (deducing it "simply appeared" before him — early hint of its agency),
  and triggers a Canterlot lockdown.
- **Р5 Перша помилка** — she smuggles it to her observatory; standard magic detects nothing;
  frustrated and trauma-triggered, she puts it on anyway. It responds only to negative emotion,
  forcibly merging with her as "reality dissolves into hatred." **The central irreversible act.**
- **Р6 Зустріч з кошмаром** — she wakes transformed (red coat, and — shockingly for an earth pony
  — a **unicorn horn**); an alien ideology reframing princesses as tyrants invades her mind; she
  annihilates attacking timberwolves and the land itself, and feels nothing.
- **Р7 Вчитель магії** — **Zecora** (in verse) heals the "dark fire" damage; unable to say her old
  name, the mare names herself **Fallenstar** from her new cutie mark (an eclipse split by a
  comet). Zecora agrees to teach control — philosophy, not offense.
- **Р8 Воля павшої** — training montage (magic-as-meaning beats academic formula); Zecora withholds
  "dark magic" and dismisses her. Fallenstar recovers her keepsakes from the ransacked observatory
  and resolves to find "a real teacher, real books" — Canterlot's School for Gifted Unicorns.

### Ч2 «В тіні Кантерлота» (In Canterlot's Shadow)
- **Р1 Старі знайомі** — under the cover **Starry Night**, she learns her parents only want her
  *found* (not punished), destabilizing her; talks into the school's advanced cohort; manipulates
  the smitten guard **Morning Rush (Ранковий Порив)** into housing her.
- **Р2 Значення слів** — Poryv's orphan backstory; she finds Zecora's teaching superior to academic
  dogma; accepts his backyard training ground.
- **Р3 Тренування для двох** — she discovers offensive magic requires channeling genuine **hatred**;
  catches herself thinking of allies as tools "to use or discard."
- **Р4 Інша точка зору** — exam before three professors incl. the elitist **Aresta**; provoked, she
  unconsciously *disintegrates* the target, terrifying the room; admitted with a warning.
- **Р5 Шкільні дні** — she notices textbooks are word-for-word identical across four centuries
  (Celestia's tenure has calcified the curriculum); a "light spell" inverts into shadow; the
  faculty forbid discussion — confirming a suppressed body of knowledge.
- **Р6 Прощання з минулим** — she learns the suppressed art is **dark magic** (Sombra-era, erased
  from record). To conquer her fear, she mind-wipes Poryv, **erases her parents' memory of her
  existence, and burns her childhood home.** Total severance from her origin.
- **Р7 Тренування для одної** — a year on: dark magic *rewrites the essential nature* of things; she
  wins a duel with Poryv; argues history has stagnated a millennium under the princesses.
- **Р8 Падіння в темряву** — Twilight guest-lectures; Fallenstar visits Ponyville (meets Spike,
  Starlight, the mirror-portal, and the Amulet that has *mysteriously reappeared* a year later —
  its own agency). Summoned before what she thinks is a tribunal, she instead meets **Крило Ночі
  (Wing of Night)** — Luna-linked secret order under **Cold of Day (Холод Дня)**; her "test" is a
  psychic ordeal (slaughter endless sorrowful illusions, then drain everyone she's loved) until
  she feels nothing. She's the only candidate who "didn't look away from the abyss." "Starry Night"
  is erased from all record; she steps through a portal to the Wing's fortress.

### Ч3 «Крило Ночі» (Wing of Night)
- **Р1 Тіні минулого** — she joins nine candidates at a ruined pre-Equestrian fortress; a lecture
  reveals the 1000-year Selestia/Luna war destabilized the **Dream World** into a nightmare realm
  of emotion-eating predators; Selestia secretly founded the Wing (using Sombra's writings) and
  covertly controls the Wonderbolts.
- **Р2 Вчитель гріхів** — reunites with Cold of Day; meets **Supernova/Nova**, master of "Flow,"
  who teaches the **umbra/lumen** distinction (umbra = extremized emotion beyond a threshold,
  requiring a "pact" with the spell). Her demo spell nearly kills the group — it turns to consume
  its creator.
- **Р3 Нові друзі** — domestic chapter; she scavenges an abandoned pegasus house into a home; bonds
  with Nova, who warns that dark mages are deliberately isolated and not to trust the handlers.
- **Р4 Вчитель страждань** — the Wing's five master "paths" (Flow, Names, Dreams, Reality, Life) =
  the **Круг (Circle)**; **Luna** is the Dream-master. Luna reads Fallenstar's fear instantly, can
  only see her *past* (not future), and formalizes her two names (Павша Зірка / Зоряна Ніч).
- **Р5 Сила всередині** — training is genuine torture because her magic comes from the parasitic
  Amulet, not her soul. Nova performs forbidden **soul-reforging**; they glimpse Fallenstar's true
  shattered soul (a grey pony impaled on shadow-crystal). Luna can fragment into weaker duplicates.
- **Р6 Пір'я крила** — she masters **shadow-step** teleportation and the "choir" (combined dark-mage
  will); her group elects her leader; first Dream-World raid earns full adept status.
- **Р7 Неминуча доля** — four years pass. She learns the first princesses were **artificial
  instruments** created by an ancient unicorn Circle — so alicorns can be replaced. **Nova is caught
  stealing forbidden texts and vanishes as a fugitive**, severing her one confidante.
- **Р8 Чорна зоря** — a Daring Do / Ahuizotl interlude glimpses her prophesied identity ("ancient
  Nightmare, Death of the World"). Then Cold of Day finds her anti-princess research; in panic she
  **kills him**, flees the Wing as a fugitive, and leaps through Twilight's mirror into the human
  world.

### Ч4 «Світ Людей» (World of Humans)
- **Р1 Перші кроки** — she lands in the Equestria-Girls human world; **Sunset Shimmer** (portal
  guardian) finds her; she poses as a runaway student but unnervingly recites classified Equestrian
  truths. Meets human Twilight (a non-royal tech genius) and the group.
- **Р2 Значення перспективи** — she keeps over-sharing forbidden lore (the sirens as Starswirl-era
  weapons; Sunset's real "Harmony spell") — alarming Sunset.
- **Р3 Брехливі визнання** — Sunset interrogates her; both admit they're lying and reach a truce.
  Twilight introduces her to modern technology.
- **Р4 Розмова мовчки** — she teaches Sunset two-way mental "color" communication (a new technique
  for this world); a détente forms.
- **Р5 Пам'ять заходу** — via the mind-link she reveals she trained under Luna; Sunset narrates her
  true backstory (stealing Sombra's writings, arrest, exile).
- **Р6 Ніч неминучого падіння** — flashback: Sunset's escape is far darker than canon — she drains
  guards with necromancy and blasts a hole through Selestia before fleeing the mirror, permanently
  scarred.
- **Р7 Доля павших** — Sunset warns against building on "banal hatred"; the two exchange true names;
  Fallenstar gifts Sunset Zecora's coded book.
- **Р8 Зламана гармонія** — time-skip: Fallenstar assimilates, concluding Equestria's imposed peace
  caused a millennium of stagnation vs. humanity's progress (hardening her ideology). Plot escalates:
  **Rarity is kidnapped** by a wealthy, magically-informed group that can *suppress the Elements*;
  the police are compromised. Cliffhanger of helplessness.

### Ч5 «Перший Гріх» (First Sin) — climax
- **Р1 Технології та магія** — the friends are powerless (Elements deactivated). Fallenstar proposes
  **fusing magic with technology** to manufacture magic; the first device works — the deliberate
  decision to "bypass the rules of reality."
- **Р2 Рука та корона** — the device becomes the wearable **Left Hand of Night**; a parallel **Crown
  of the West** (a Sunset-name pun) half-triggers Sunset's demon form, which she learns to control.
  They gather reinforcements for a rescue raid.
- **Р3 Нова гармонія** — three-pronged assault on the mansion of three "entrepreneurs"; first
  bloodshed; they find Rarity/Pinkie/Fluttershy wired to draining machines; the antagonists wield a
  stolen-Element "choir."
- **Р4 Сила диявола** — brutal duel: Fallenstar kills one antagonist and is nearly killed; Sunset
  fully transforms into her demon and defeats the rest (torturing one to a broken mind). *The
  strongest candidate for the titular "first sin": the "good guys" become killers and torturers.*
- **Р5 Старі друзі** — aftermath; students fear Sunset's demon form; they free the captives and
  discover another pod holding **Nova**; they improvise an emergency portal to evacuate.
- **Р6 Затишшя після бурі** — recovery; the media frames it as gang violence; the friends debate the
  morality of the violence; Sunset doubts whether Fallenstar's arrival caused it all.
- **Р7 Доброта перед злом** — Fallenstar heals Nova; fragments of her mission ("know and defeat the
  princesses") surface; she quotes the Arizotla prophecy over herself; declines the group's offer of
  permanent belonging ("I'll think about it").
- **Р8 Друга наднова** — they restore Nova's mind (preserving shared memories). Nova's backstory
  (Master of the Flow, the Circle, Cold of Day's betrayal, capture & torture = the antagonists'
  techno-magic origin). She reveals natural **portals are "world-lines"** and maps another near
  Canterlot.
- **Р9 Зворотній бік світу** — they build a redirect device to reroute the natural portal into
  Twilight's Equestrian mirror; a letter through it is answered by **Starlight Glimmer**, noting
  Twilight has been "busy" (cliffhanger seed). Nova moves in with Twilight.
- **Р10 Вибір павшої** — Fallenstar secretly departs, redirects the *ancient* portal tied to her
  pendant (a Sombra-era battlefield erased into a void), and steps into a dead, ruined pocket-world.
  She leaves a recorded farewell for Sunset ("Don't fear your own reflection, friend") and her two
  artifacts behind — self-exiling to "the only place the Cursed Star can call home." **End of Book 1.**

## THE REBELLION — RU «Часть 2–3» (written in RU, NOT yet translated)

> Note: `ideas/Stories/FIE/Fragment I Structure.md` gives an *evolved plan* whose chapter names
> differ slightly from the written monolith; below follows the **written manuscript**. Saari and
> Moonlight-as-Princess-of-Destruction do **not** appear in the written Часть 2–3 — they belong to
> drafted finale/future material (`Chrysalis encounter.md`, `Princesses' crown.md`).

### Часть 2 «Восстание против Мира» (Rebellion against the World)
1. **Начало Конца** — Twilight, overwhelmed governing the annexed South, is coached by Luna toward
   decentralized rule; **Manehattan is destroyed by a dark rift** (First Catastrophe); an injured
   **Moonlight Shadow** arrives.
2. **Битва без Смысла** — the **Fallen Stars (Падшие Звёзды)** under the unseen **Fallenstar** are
   named; Vanhoover secedes for techno-magic; **Cloudsdale is destroyed**; Moonlight joins the group.
3. **Кристальный шторм** — flight to the storm-sealed Crystal Empire (the Crystal Heart in overload);
   Moonlight is revealed able to wield umbra without corruption.
4. **Падение** — Equestria fractures; the South formally secedes as **ОСП (Union of Free Ponies)**;
   a Fallen mission reveals **Chrysalis** (with Grogar's Bell) has built a life-devouring swarm
   marching on Equestria; Twilight departs with the Fallen to verify.
5. **За Пределами** — aboard the ship **Ласточка (Swallow)**, Twilight is well-treated and begins
   umbral lessons under Nova; portal-travel to the northern outpost Omega. The Fallen are governed by
   a **Круг (Circle)**; Fallenstar holds the ceremonial title "First Star" but pursues "her own war."
6. **Северная Пустошь** — Asteria Vella claims ponies "already lost" an older cosmic war and all
   princesses are unwitting instruments; Twilight reads Fallenstar's notes (star-cores reverse-
   engineered from the Crystal Heart and Tree of Harmony).
7. **Проклятый лёд** — in Queen Platinum's buried castle they wake the **Windigo** — "Guardian of
   Fate," an extinction entity that *escalates against any resistance*; the Swallow is shot down;
   Fallenstar guides the rescue telepathically. A new storm re-forms as Part 2 ends.

### Часть 3 «Восстание против Смысла» (Rebellion against Meaning)
1. **Шанс измениться** — Nova reveals umbral mastery slowly dissolves a mage into a mindless
   shadow-wraith (like Sombra); the Ninth Group over-destroys a town saving it from ОСП pirates
   (Part 3's moral question); a Radical horde marches on Ponyville.
2. **Гибель Уверенности** — Ponyville's defense; "Moonlight" co-runs tactics with Twilight; her
   faith in clean heroism erodes.
3. **Гибель Наивности** — **"Moonlight" is revealed to be Fallenstar**; **Selestia duels Fallenstar**
   (who wields the Amulet and summons the sword **Слабость / Weakness**); Luna halts it, naming
   Fallenstar **"Godslayer."** Twilight dons the Amulet, is corrupted, and forces a duel that tears
   open a reality-devouring **Void**.
4. **Нежеланная Правда** — the true catastrophe: **Twilight erased her own alicorn nature — taking
   Celestia, Luna, Cadance & Flurry Heart out of existence.** Harmony is dead. Fallenstar relocates
   the group to her extra-dimensional fortress **Нексус (Nexus)** and confesses her role.
5. **Свет изнутри** — in timeless Nexus she shows a frozen view of "the end of the world"; she
   doesn't know her own birth name; **confirms she has been Moonlight all along** (a twist) and can
   take human form; recruits Starlight & Pinkie to rebuild replica Elements.
6. **Вопросы без Ответов** — they forge crystal replicas of five Elements via redefining each one's
   true meaning; the sixth (Magic) needs the originals from the Tree — forcing a return to the Void.
7. **Призраки Прошлого** — into the Void; shadow-doppelgangers weaponize the friends' insecurities;
   they retrieve the Tree of Harmony trunk. *Lore:* Selestia founded the Wing (and Wonderbolts) to
   fight nightmares from an ancient "Crisis of Dreams."
8. **Изменение Пути** — the Void-wound will consume Equestria "by end of this cycle," then the
   planet; true healing needs all four (absent) princesses; the stopgap is a castle + Fallen-relay
   perimeter to *slow* it. **Moonlight Shadow is confirmed a real, separate character** on an
   unresolved artifact-quest.
9. **Сердце Звезды** *(final written chapter)* — in **Alpha**, the Fallen Stars' underground capital
   (built over the "Village of Equality"); internal schism and rank-and-file hostility to the Mane 6.
   The manuscript's **last line breaks off mid-dialogue** (Starlight testing a mind-link with Pinkie).

### Часть 4 «Выбор» — a one-line stub
"Becoming the Princess of Destruction, finding the solution, confrontation with the Sleeping
Alicorn…" — followed by a bare character-glossary of ~30 names for unwritten chapters. The evolved
plan (`Fragment I Structure.md`) renames this arc **«Осколок последнего Огня»** (Ветер Хаоса /
Лунный Свет / Принцесса Разрушения) + an epilogue "Legend of the Sleeping Alicorn."

## Planned finale (from `ideas/` notes — drafted, ahead of the manuscript)
Fallenstar abdicates **Destruction to Moonlight Shadow**, forges a soul-bound treaty binding all
princesses (the `Princesses' crown` scene — Celestia had fallen to her shadow **Daybreaker**), and
becomes a **Rider** of the new Time-princess **Twilight Ash**. Fragments **II Time** and
**III Eternity** exist only as outlines.

**The full planned arc through the end of the saga** — Fallenstar finding Eterna's memory, breaking the
world's dream-cycle, the reveal that the *real* world died in her own techno-magic rebellion, taming the
Windigos/Cultivators, the starship **Крило Принцеси**, and the **final war against «Death» (the mad
Creator)** — is laid out in [deep-history-and-endgame.md](deep-history-and-endgame.md) (§C), together with
the **Four Riders** eschatology (§B) and the pony prehistory (§A).
