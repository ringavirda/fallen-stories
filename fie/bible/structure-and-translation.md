# Structure & the RU→UK reorganization

## The hierarchy shift

You promoted every structural level up by one and inserted a new bottom layer — because
readers found a single "Часть" too long for one book.

- **RU:** `Fragment → Часть (Part) → subsection → [implicit scene-breaks]`
- **UK:** `Fragment → Книга (Book) → Частина (Part) → Розділ (Chapter)`

A RU **Часть became a UK Книга**; RU subsections became UK **Частини**; and an explicit
**Розділ** (chapter) layer was added. The **Fragment** level is preserved — the UK title doc
still reads *«Фрагмент І – Вогонь → Книга 0, Книга 1»*.

In the RU original, chapter breaks were not headings at all: they were **short italic
one-line epigraphs** (defined in the notation guide as "special lines… for logical division
within a chapter"), e.g. *«Выбор пути – самый опасный из всех решений…»*. The UK promotes one
such epigraph to a **quoted subtitle** under each numbered Розділ, and consolidates 2–3
consecutive RU epigraph-sections into one named chapter.

**Load-bearing proof that Ч5 = Немыслимое alone (not a 3-way merge):** UK Ч5's final chapter
«Вибір павшої» ends on the exact sentence that ends RU «Немыслимое» — *«…покинула дом Сансет
Шиммер… единственным местом, которое Фолленстар сможет назвать своим домом…»* → UK *«…єдиним
місцем, що **Проклята Зірка** зможе назвати своїм домом…»* — immediately before the RU
`## Проклятая Миром` heading. **UK Book 1 terminates precisely at the Немыслимое boundary.**
Nice touch: the UK renames "Fallenstar" → **«Проклята Зірка» (Cursed Star)** in that last line,
foreshadowing the next book's likely title, «Проклята».

## What changed in translation (you edited while translating)

The edits fall into two very different buckets.

### A) Structural / paratextual — large and deliberate (this is where the "editing" lives)
- Часть → Книга promotion + a new Розділ layer.
- **Truncating Book 1 at the Немыслимое boundary** and deferring two *written* RU subsections
  to a future book — the single most consequential editorial decision.
- A **brand-new Р0 «Легенда про Принцесу»** overture that reframes the whole book around the
  fallen-princess / eternal-cycle motif before the reader meets the protagonist.
- Appendices externalized into a structured **Book 0 «Додатки»** (Знання / Пам'ять / Розуміння)
  plus new standalone extras (Абсолютний Вибір, Колода карт Темряви, Мунлайт, Тір-ліст принцес).
- Freshly-composed chapter epigraphs where RU had none (e.g. Ч2/Р1 «Велике місто малих поні…»).

### B) Sentence-level — light and faithful (~80–90% close translation)
- Prose maps almost paragraph-for-paragraph; the prologue is ~95% literal. **Not a heavy rewrite.**
- Purposeful micro-enrichments: added time anchors; **naming "Harmony"** as the girls' power
  source where RU was vague; a consistent **"alicorn" → "princess"** motif reweighting
  (RU «странный отпечаток» → UK «відбиток **принцеси**»; RU closing «созвездие **аликорна**» →
  UK «сузір'я **принцеси**»).
- Part rename **«Немыслимое» ("Unthinkable") → «Перший Гріх» ("First Sin")** — moralizing the
  techno-magic genesis as a *fall/sin* rather than an epistemic taboo.
- **Verse is genuinely re-authored** — Zecora's rhymes are re-rhymed in Ukrainian with preserved
  meter, not glossed.
- Names UK-phonologized (Звездопад → Зорепад, Сверхновая → Наднова). **No age / motivation /
  event reorderings** were found inside the shared text.

### C) Canon in flight (independent of translation)
Your worldbuilding is mid-revision, so some documents genuinely disagree (cosmology layers,
Wendigo origin, how alicorns are made, the power-letter scale). See

## Chapter sizing (for reference)

UK Розділи run **~1,200–3,300 words each** (mean ≈ 2,200; 4–9 pages) — a 10–15-min read,
ideal for serialized online reading. Частини bundle 8–10 chapters into ~16–25k-word arcs;
Book 1 totals ~100k words (a full novel). The only outlier is Р0 (~375 w), an intentional
overture. This sizing is well-judged.
