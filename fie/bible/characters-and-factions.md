# Characters & Factions

## The protagonist — three forms

Family **Starfall / Звездопад / Зорепад** (earth-pony naming scheme). Cutie mark: a **star falling
against an eclipse** (earlier: a winding path paved with stars).

| Form | Race / age | Power | Known as | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fading Starfall** (Зникаючий Зорепад / «Фейд») `[Зз]/[Из]` | earth, ~16 | κ (kappa) | "Daughter of the Starfall family" | the curious innocent; court-astronomer family |
| **Starry Night** (Зоряна Ніч / Звёздная Ночь) | unicorn, ~24 | δ (delta) | "Mage of the Wing of Night," "Walker Between Worlds," "Traitor" | cover identity; Wing of Night dark mage |
| **Fallenstar** (Фолленстар / Павша Зірка) `[Фс]` | half-alicorn → alicorn | α → Ω | "Princess of Destruction," "Godslayer," "Predicted Curse," "first Star" | founder of the Fallen Stars; aspect of **Destruction/Choice** |

**Arc:** childhood curiosity → Zecora's tutelage → discovers dark magic, betrays parents & a friend,
joins the Wing of Night → kills Cold of Day, flees to the **human world** (bonds with Sunset, discovers
technology) → invents **techno-magic** → self-exile → (untranslated) builds a base beyond Harmony,
learns weaving from **Lilith**, forges the sword **Weakness**, founds the **Fallen Stars**, triggers
the Manehattan Catastrophe & the **End of the World** → duels Selestia, "Godslayer" → post-war
abdicates Destruction **to Moonlight** and becomes the **Rider** of Twilight Ash. She persistently
**refuses the title "princess."**

## Core cast

- **Moonlight Shadow (Мунлайт Шадоу)** `[Мш]` — the *second* **Princess of Destruction** (of the
  northern **Dominion of Destruction**). Umbral natural talent; wields a **living scythe that is
  Saari**. Served as regent of Equestria while Fallenstar fought the world. Fallenstar "touched her
  fate with fire," deliberately created as her contingency successor. Past: a Canterlot unicorn
  student of Celestia's school, umbral talent, traumatized by her own magic.
- **Saari (Саарі)** `[Са]` — a "higher shadow (тінь)" that consumed its own soul; Fallenstar's sword
  **Weakness** severed Saari from her original soul and re-bound her to **Moonlight** as a living
  weapon (scythe / shadow-claws). Playful, provoking. *(Absent from the written Часть 2–3; appears in
  drafted finale scenes.)*
- **Supernova / Nova (Сверхновая / Супернова)** `[Сн]` — unicorn dark mage, **Master of the Flow**;
  Fallenstar's mentor and only true friend in the Wing; created the base **Deck of Darkness** (Tarot).
  Exiled as a fugitive; later captured/tortured (origin of the antagonists' techno-magic); rehabilitated.
- **Twilight Sparkle (Твайлайт)** `[Тс]` — current **Princess of Friendship/Value** (tier D — youngest,
  deliberately underpowered). Corrupted by the Amulet, she erases her own alicorn nature and all reigning
  alicorns from existence. **[revision]** later becomes bearer of the sands of memory, takes the vacant
  **Time throne**, and renames herself **Twilight Ash (Твайлайт Аш)**. A separate old-world *sleeping*
  Twilight is the "Sleeping Princess" of the legend.
- **Sunset Shimmer (Сансет Шиммер)** `[Сш]` — human-world deuteragonist and Fallenstar's mirror-image;
  a radicalized exile with a controllable demon form (Crown of the West). A fallen unicorn noble house.
- **Zecora (Зекора)** `[Зе]` — verse-speaking mystic; teaches magic-as-meaning; withholds "dark magic."
- **Morning Rush / Gust (Ранковий Порив / Утренний Порыв)** `[Рп]` — orphaned pegasus guard lieutenant,
  smitten with the protagonist across identities; her moral foil, repeatedly manipulated.
- **Cold of Day (Холод Дня)** `[Хд]` — leader of the Wing of Night recruitment; loves Fallenstar; she
  kills him in Ч3/Р8.
- **Professor Aresta (Ареста)** `[Ар]` — rigid academic gatekeeper, later implied a Wing watcher.

## Royal sisters & Cadance

- **Celestia (Селестія)** `[Се]` — aspect **Day/Absolute** (S). Dons the **Armor of the West**, becoming
  an unstoppable war-goddess; briefly falls to her shadow **Daybreaker (Дейбрейкер)** during the End.
  Fallenstar names her the main obstacle and brings a god-slaying weapon from beyond Harmony.
- **Luna (Луна)** `[Лу]` — aspect **Night/Changes** (SS). Master of Dream-magic in the Wing; can fragment
  into weaker duplicates; **sides with Fallenstar**.
- **Cadance / Mi Amore Cadenza (Мі Аморе Каденза)** `[Ка]` — aspect **Love/Sacrifice** (B); relies on the
  Crystal Heart rather than draining subjects.

## Key artifacts

- **Weakness (Слабість / Слабость)** — a god-slaying blade from beyond Harmony; a human-scaled hilt,
  crescent guard, cracked one-edged blade of "materialized indifference" with a blood-crystal; distorts
  space. *(Strange Tales retcons its origin: forged from a branch of the burnt Tree of Memory + a "Seed
  of Evil.")*
- **Amulet of the Alicorn (Амулет Алікорна)** — a cursed-steel artifact of near-limitless power with its
  own will; the engine of Fallenstar's transformation and of Twilight's corruption.
- **Armor of the West / Radiance of Dawn** — Celestia's godlike princess-armor & golden glaive.
- **Blood crystals / crystallized void (криваві кристали)** — Fallenstar's unique crystals, the only ones
  that hold above-"dark" spells; now power the princess palaces.
- **Left Hand of Night / Crown of the West** — the techno-magic calibrators built in Book 1 Ч5.

# Factions

- **Equestria / the Old Kingdom** — ruled by the princesses via the advisory **Circle (Круг)**. Military =
  **Army of Day (Армия Дня)** (star-steel plate, melee, low-tech, monopoly on star steel) + **Wing of
  Night (Крыло Ночи)**, Luna's secret dream-walking dark mages using **cursed steel** (and covertly the
  Wonderbolts).
- **ОСП — Union of Free Ponies (Объединение Свободных Пони)** — the South's secessionist bloc of nomadic
  airship fleets / mercenary traders; main black-market source of "star tech." Named: Warm Blizzard,
  Tilius the Old, Braeburn.
- **Radicals (Радикалы)** — a catch-all for warbands, cults, and marauders driven mad by the spreading
  corruption; framed as "my fault" (Fallenstar's guilt).
- **Fallen Stars (Падшие Звёзди / Падші Зірки)** — Fallenstar's techno-magic army; "outcasts who took up
  arms against Fate." Elite = **Battle Stars**. Numbered cells (e.g. the **Ninth Group**), standing groups
  Alpha/Theta/Omega, and the secret **First/Prime Group**. Linked by the telepathic **infosphere**.
  Old Equestrians nickname them "Legion." "Neither villains nor heroes."
- **Dominion of Destruction (Доминион Разрушения)** — the northern realm beyond the ice wastes, ruled by
  **Moonlight Shadow** as Princess of Destruction.
- **New Equestria** — the post-war coalition (modeled on the outside galaxy's **Galactic Coalition**)
  uniting Old Equestria, the Crystal Empire, the Alliance of the South, and the Dominion.
- **Beyond Harmony** — the **Galactic Coalition of Sentient Races**, Free Traders, Ranger Corps, raiders,
  and ultimately **Lilith** — the looming existential threat.
