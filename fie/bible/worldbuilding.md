## 1. Cosmology & metaphysics [revision]

- **False angels (хибні янголи)** — ancient energy-beings, originally as material as starlight, drawn to
  the galaxy by **emotion**. They clothed themselves in mortals' myths and fears (hence "angels"), losing
  their native form but keeping one power: **weaving (ткацтво)** — reshaping reality by will. Immortal but
  **cannot reproduce**, so they are fixated on decoding life.
- **The Creator (Творець) & the Tree of Memory** — a "gardener/engineer" angel storing all understanding in
  the Tree. His creation **Lilith (Лилит)** opened herself to be woven into; together they discovered
  **love**.
- **Original sin** — the Tree's knowledge was already poisoned by mortal malice; love let Lilith absorb it,
  turning her soul insatiable. She forged the **first blade** and killed the Creator — an **eternal wound**
  in the world. The Tree withered; every linked angel was corrupted → the galaxy-burning **War of the
  Angels**. Lilith becomes the **Last Angel**, seeding new life carrying **"angel's blood"** (the capacity
  to weave); most such beings resemble her — **humanity**.
- **Harmony (Гармонія)** — Equestria's pocket-reality, born from the Creator's fallen "gift branch"
  catalyzed by Lilith's life-wave. From outside: a purple energy-storm that destroys any ship. Inside: many
  **bubble-worlds linked by threads like leaves on a tree** (no direct travel; hence portals). Worlds are
  **dual** — Creator's ideal forms + Lilith's distortion = Equestria's fairy-tale-nightmare quality.
- **Two world-substances:** **"sands of memory"** (from the gift branch; carry fragments of the first
  Time-princess's mind) and **"dark fire / fire of creation"** (Lilith's life-force). Both are being
  **exhausted**, so the protective storm is dying — which will open Harmony to the galaxy (and Lilith).

## 2. Magic system

Two orthogonal axes. **Levels** (like states of matter):

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **automata (автомата)** | lowest; spontaneous, uncontrolled (dragons, earth ponies, enchanted items) |
| **lumen (люмен)** | ordinary directed "light" magic (unicorns, pegasi, changelings) |
| **umbra (умбра)** | overloaded "dark" magic — spells become *alive*, persist after the caster, spawn ghosts/timberwolves; can rewrite an object's essence; needs sustained "overcharged emotion" |
| **crudelia (круделіа)** | "anti-natural" rule-breaking that commands reality (Discord, Harmony, alicorn princesses) |

**Types** (schools, orthogonal to levels): **chaotic** (ritual, no cause/effect — Discord = its crudelia);
**harmonic** (sums exceeding parts — pegasus weather-teams; the **Magic of Friendship** is an *artificial*
harmonic spell); **distorted/curved** (unicorn will-magic — the best-studied).

- A pony's **race = the ratio of energies within it**; an **alicorn** has all three tribal magics equally
  strong. Magic is a single "magic field," a variation of the immaterium, arising from the **soul's**
  friction against fundamental aspects.
- **Enchanting:** star steel holds spells; **living crystals** conduct/store magic. **Princess palaces are
  macro-scale magic machines** (Twilight's broadcasts Magic-of-Friendship to reduce conflict; the Crystal
  Empire stabilizes reality and drains residents' life-force).

## 3. Princesses & thrones

A **princess ≠ an alicorn.** An alicorn is just a *body-form* (three magics balanced) able to survive an
aspect crashing onto it — anyone skilled enough can become alicorn-shaped, gaining no powers. (The *original*
alicorns were made by the Circle as a **perfect magical hybrid** of the three tribes — the same craft as
cockatrices/bugbears, but so deep it produced **true new life**; see
[deep-history-and-endgame.md](deep-history-and-endgame.md).) A **princess** is the living **embodiment of an
aspect**, holding a **throne** in Harmony's weave, earned by *creating a new meaning* (sacrificing one's
future). **Eterna was a princess, not (merely) an alicorn** — she **created the first throne**, anchoring the
very concept of **Time**. Vacant thrones malfunction → catastrophes, so **Fate keeps thrones
filled**. Princesses have two advantages: control over their aspect (unstoppable) and access to the
**ethereal plane** (editing past & future). They can be killed, but the throne persists; truly ending an
aspect requires "burning out both frayed ends" from history and possibility.

| Princess | Aspect (public / deeper) | Created | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Etherna → Twilight Ash** | Time / Wholeness | cutie marks & Destiny | SSR |
| **Luna** | Night / Changes | the "beyond"; can summon void-forces | SS |
| **Celestia** | Day / Absolute | reality itself ("Power") | S |
| **Cadance** | Love / Sacrifice | Hope | B |
| **Twilight Sparkle** | Friendship / Value | a new kind of Magic | D |
| **Fallenstar → Moonlight** | **Destruction / Choice** | "an end with its own meaning" | SS |

- **Time + Day** are the true co-rulers ("they will continue to exist"). The original Time-princess
  **Eterna** was **killed** by a hate-twisted Destruction + Friendship — which plunged the world into the
  **dream/sleep** the saga inhabits.
- **The dead Time-throne & wild time (key mechanic).** Inside the dream (i.e. most of the story world, up to
  Fallenstar's reunion with the old-world Twilight), the true Princess of Time is dead; only a **memory-image
  of Eterna persists**, because the dreaming Twilight **cannot imagine a world without her**. With no real
  Time-princess, **time runs wild — diverging between worlds** (this is why far more time passed in Equestria
  than in the human world, as **Sunset** noticed). When the **old-world Twilight Sparkle takes the name
  Twilight Ash and claims the Throne of Time**, this **stabilizes time and re-synchronizes it** across worlds.
- **Destruction** is anathema to Reality/Wholeness; its only true death is **self-destruction** (the supreme
  form of destruction, which would erase even fundamental aspects). It tacitly does not oppose Night.
- **The Riders.** Time takes **Riders** — bearers of end-forces — to guard Harmony. There are **four**, with
  **Twilight Ash as their leader**; **Fallenstar is her Rider** (bearing the sword *Слабкість*), following
  Ash's will to destroy what must be destroyed. The Riders are **amoral** — they do what is necessary. Full
  framework in [deep-history-and-endgame.md](deep-history-and-endgame.md#b-the-four-riders--ridersmd).

## 4. Timeline / eras

An **Epoch → Era** system. Your example **"5Э 025" = Epoch of Harmony, Era 5 ("Era of Dusk/Twilight"),
year 025**.

- **Epoch of the First (Ea):** Dawn → Garden (the Tree/Lilith/original sin) → Fall (War of the Angels).
- **Epoch of Those Who Came After (Eb):** Colonization (~10 000 y) → Coalition (~600 y, ongoing outside).
- **Epoch of Harmony (Э / Ec):** Э1 Creation (first Time-princess) → Э2 Queens (Grogar's War of Monsters,
  Windigos, migration south) → Era of the Circle (Wars of Unification; the Circle creates Celestia & Luna)
  → Э3 Princesses (Nightmare Moon) → Э4 Celestia (the long enforced peace; Twilight; the Rebellion / near-
  End) → **Э5 Dusk (present)**.
- Cross-world dates are unreliable **because the Time-princess is dead and her throne empty** — the in-
  universe justification for portal/timeline-hopping.
- **Beneath all of this** sits the pre-Harmony/first-civilization deep history (the Creator's ponies, their
  world-machine empire, its fall, and the Windigos-as-Cultivators) — see
  [deep-history-and-endgame.md](deep-history-and-endgame.md). **«Смерть» (Death)** — the mad Creator's
  consciousness — is the saga's ultimate antagonist, and the **Four Riders** (Amulet / Fire of Creation /
  Weakness / Sands of Time) are the force that slows it.

## 5. Materials & techno-magic

- **Equestria:** **star steel / star iron** (an "impossible alloy" that absorbs/dissipates magic; forged
  with dark magic; princess monopoly) and its inverse **cursed steel** (feeds life-energy to the wielder;
  a second "horn" for mages; the Amulet is cursed steel).
- **Fallen Stars — techno-magic:** replicators, light-furnaces, **"false steel" (hyperentropic matter)**,
  **living crystals** (green/blue/red/white/black — different emotional resonances; white = superconductor,
  black = high-capacity/unstable), **energy cells** & **star-cores** (copies of the Crystal Heart), the
  **infosphere** (their telepathic network), and **magic calibrators** (casters, accelerators, exo-armor).
  Fallenstar's unique materials: **blood crystals / crystallized void** and **"materialized indifference"**
  (from the sword Weakness).
- Thematic law (`Armory.md` epigraph): *"Technology and arcana must never be mixed, or doom befalls the
  world that becomes home to this unforgivable sin."* → the **"First Sin."**

## 6. Tarot — the Deck of Darkness

Supernova's 15-card base deck (Locations / Paths / Aspects / Divine) + Fallenstar's 9-card extension (Three
Peoples, Alicorn, Fabric, Chaos, Maiden of Fire, Maiden of Darkness, Nightmare, Ship, Blade). A dark-cast

## 7. Notation {#notation}

Three categories of typographic marking (from `Спеціальні нотації`):

- **Text:** plain = narration; `…thought…` = internal thought; `"standalone quote"` = scene/section divider;
  `"inline"` = emphasis / indirect speech.
- **Dialogue:** `[Xx] –` = a direct line by a 2-letter-coded speaker; `— line, — said X.` = direct speech
  with attribution; rhymed `[Ze] –` = Zecora. Unknown speaker = `[?]`.
- **Speaker codes `[**]`:** first letters of the parts of an earth-scheme name (Twilight Sparkle → **Тс**;
  Shadow Moonlight → **Мш**), or first two letters of a one-part neo-name (Rarity → **Ра**; Zecora → **Зе**).

## 8. Pony naming schemes

Three systems (`Схеми імен поні` / `Pony names.md`): **ancient/unicorn** (family-first, matriarchal —
nobility); **new/earth** (personal-first, patriarchal); **free/pegasus** (descriptive phrase, nothing
inherited). Single-word **neo-names** mark the young or low-born. Applications: Celestia & Luna's one-part
names imply *artificial creation*; **Starfall** uses the earth scheme (an ancestor would be "Starfall
Dreamer"); **Fallenstar** is a neo-name "carrying the promise of the future."
