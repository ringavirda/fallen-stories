## What FIE is

A **dark science-fiction reimagining of *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic***. It keeps
the cast and setting but systematically demystifies the fantasy: dark magic becomes a
defined energy state, princesses become **embodiments of reality-aspects** (not goddesses),
myths become suppressed history, and miracles get industrialized as **techno-magic**. The
engine of the whole work is the collision of wholesome MLP staples against blood, betrayal,
apocalypse, and cosmic horror.

The saga is planned as three **Fragments** — **I «Вогонь / Fire»**, **II «Час / Time»**,
**III «Вічність / Eternity»** — after the three central aspects. Only **Fragment I "Fire"**
is substantially written (~365,000 RU words) and it is **unfinished** (breaks off mid-chapter
in the Rebellion arc). The project is mid-migration from the RU `.docx` originals to an
**edited Ukrainian re-translation** in Google Docs; **UK Book 1 is complete**.

## The one-paragraph premise

Equestria has known 1000+ cycles of enforced peace — but "life has turned into dreams": the
world is literally a **dream** cast by a dying princess of **Time** to delay an apocalypse.
An "old soul" reincarnating across lives is born as the earth-pony filly **Fading Starfall**;
obsessed with why magic is denied to earth ponies, she bonds a cursed **Amulet of the
Alicorn**, becomes the unicorn dark-mage **Starry Night**, then the half-alicorn **Fallenstar**
— the aspect of **Destruction/Choice**, a "Godslayer" who invents **techno-magic** (the
"First Sin"), triggers the **End of the World**, and ultimately becomes the **Rider** of the
new Time-princess, **Twilight Ash**.

## Central themes

- **"Technology and arcana must never be mixed"** — techno-magic as literal "First Sin";
  saving the world from one apocalypse dooms it to a worse one.
- **The world is a dream** — a sleeping princess, an eternal cycle, "Awakening is near."
- **Identity erosion** — the protagonist's serial renaming tracks her loss of self; memory
  erasure is a recurring weapon.
- **Benevolent tyranny** — the princesses' 1000-year peace as stagnation and censorship.
- **Found family vs. self-imposed isolation**; **light/dark as a false binary** (umbra = extreme
  emotion, not evil).
