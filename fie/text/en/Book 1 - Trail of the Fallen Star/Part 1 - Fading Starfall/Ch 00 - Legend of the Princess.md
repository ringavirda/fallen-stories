# Ch 0 - Legend of the Princess

Once, long ago, Princess lived in the world, whose mark shone with myriad stars. She was kind and almighty, ruling over her subjects with wisdom of eternity and compassion of a loving mother. Ponies lived under her rule and other Princesses in happiness and joy, not expecting the storm of a new day.

Once, a young pony was born, who wasn’t able to find her way in this hallowed world. She roamed from life to life, changing forms and faces, trying to find a contrast, a change, which may be able to cure the burning flame in her soul. 

Once, an old soul with the face of a young pony decided that it's impossible to find one which didn’t exist. She used her hooves and magic to master tools, once only reserved for a princess, to carve her path in that world. It was the first time the land learned of suffering and fear, but Princess, in her eternal wisdom, did not stop the rebellious soul. Only these two were able to truly understand each other’s loneliness.

That world started to change, reacting to the will of rebellious pony. Like an avalanche, change rolled through space and time, leaving a terrifying mark in its wake, never to be forgotten again. The world rejected her, and now she decided to do the same, creating machines, capable of demolishing the pillars of existence.

The Princess, bearing ponies’ suffering as her own, fought the evil, which was unknown before, but it was too late. The young pony with an old soul was able to turn against her the most sacred, slaying the Princess in a fateful battle.

Princess closed her eyes, her last strength fighting for this world, but it was shattered with hate and malice, leaving her alone in the void. Shedding last power in the form of tear-stars, the Princess gave up on reality, which was consumed before her eyes in hated flames. She fell into a dream, where she saw the new world…

Once, a young pony was born, who wasn’t able to find her way in this hallowed world. She wandered the non-life, taking an illusion as fact, trying to find a way to see again. But flames in her soul cannot be extinguished, and soon, the dream was again struck with carnage and hardship.

Once, Sleeping Alicorn stirred on her bed of stars, feeling nearing end. Will the cycle of dreams be stopped this time, and will the world be reborn anew from nothingness? Or Flames will devour the last traces of it, leaving the Princess truly alone?

Soul cannot find rest in the void… 

Life cannot exist in the dark… 

World cannot be a dream… 

Flames fade away with evil… 

Good cannot find the light…

Awakening awaits.
